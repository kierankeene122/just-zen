# Just Zen app library

100 familiar business and personal apps, hand-selected for everyday use. This is a curated selection, not a measured global popularity ranking.

Each entry has a bundled PNG in assets/app-icons. The picker and sidebar use these files without network access. The sources.json file records download sources. scripts/download-icons.py refreshes the assets using public favicon services at build time. Brand artwork belongs to its respective owner.

Previously saved apps are retained even when removed from the catalogue. Custom websites use their declared favicon or /favicon.ico, cached locally. Service availability and embedded login compatibility vary.
