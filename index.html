Memory

Apps you are not looking at can be put to sleep: their page is closed and its memory released, then it reloads when you open it again. Sleeping apps do not show new unread counts until then. Right-click an app in the sidebar to give it its own setting.

Notifications, calls and downloads

Apps notify you through macOS. If nothing ever appears, check that Just Zen is allowed under System Settings → Notifications. Calls in Slack, Google Chat and Meet ask for your camera and microphone once per app.

Saved passwords

When you sign in to a website, Just Zen offers to remember the login. Saved logins are encrypted with your Mac's Keychain and filled in when you click a login field on the same site.

Session and data controls

Clearing a browser profile signs its websites out on this Mac. Each browser app has its own profile.
