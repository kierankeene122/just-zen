Messages come to you

One bell. Every chat. The message itself.

Slack, Google Chat, Teams and WhatsApp keep running in the background, every workspace of them. The bell shows who said what. Click it and that conversation opens in the pane you're in, not somewhere else.

Claude reads what's open

Pick a pane. Ask.

Claude Code sits beside your apps. Choose any open pane, document or note from the About dropdown and ask: summarise this thread, draft a reply, turn it into tasks. It runs inside a sandbox and only sees what you point it at.

Why not just browser tabs?

Because tabs are where attention goes to die.

Each app keeps its own login

Two Gmail accounts, three Slack workspaces, a client's Notion: all signed in at once, none of them seeing each other.

Side by side, not one at a time

Two, three or four panes, resized by dragging. The brief next to the inbox, the doc next to the chat.

One notification stream

Every chat tool in one bell, with the message text, muted when you say so.

Nothing leaves your machine

No account, no sync, no telemetry. Logins in the Keychain. Claude in a sandbox. Open source, so you can check.

Less mental load. Less app-switching chaos.
