A calm workspace for Mac

Everything you work in.
One window.

Your apps, your files, your notes and tasks, with Claude Code working alongside you. Tabs in every app, two or four apps side by side, and anything you select can go to Claude or straight onto your task list. Nothing to switch to, nowhere else to go. Just flow.

Why one window

Switching between apps is where the day disappears.

The message, the file it's about, the note you're writing and the assistant helping you, all on screen at once.

All your apps in one place

A library of a hundred web apps, or any website. Each lives in the sidebar with unread counts and opens in its own pane.

  • ⌘K jumps anywhere
  • Tabs in every app, a Browser pinned at the top
  • Own login per app; Personal and Work profiles when you want them

Claude as your copilot

Claude Code in a pane beside your apps, with the folder you give it. Select text anywhere, press ⌘⇧A, and it's in front of Claude.

  • Chat, or the real Terminal
  • Starts Notes-only; you widen access
  • Every command shown to you first

Notes, documents and tasks, same window

An infinite whiteboard, Markdown notes with wiki links, PDFs and Word files, and a task list that stays visible.

  • ⌘⇧T turns any selection into a task
  • Annotate PDFs, edit DOCX, save copies
  • All local, Keychain-encrypted
Send anything, from anywhere, to Claude

Select it. Right-click. Summarise, reply, or turn it into tasks.

An email in Gmail, a thread in Slack, a paragraph in a PDF, a note you're writing, output in the terminal: select any of it, right-click or press ⌘⇧A, and it lands in front of Claude with one-click actions. Summarise it, draft a reply, turn it into tasks, or ask your own question. Or skip Claude altogether: ⌘⇧T turns the selection straight into a task. No copying and pasting between tools, and nothing is sent until you choose.

You stay in control. Claude only sees the folder you connect, runs inside an operating-system sandbox, and asks before it runs a command or touches anything outside your notes.

Room to work

Two apps side by side. Four when you need them. Tabs in all of them.

Put the brief next to the design, or four dashboards in a grid, and switch from the header whenever the work changes.

Tabs everywhere

Two inboxes, several Slack workspaces, a few Notion pages. ⌘T opens one, ⌘W closes it.

A real Browser, pinned

First in the sidebar, for everything that isn't an app. Links from notes and chat open here.

Groups with a face

Related apps behind one icon you choose. Click it and they fan out, unread counts included.

Apps that sleep

Hidden apps close after a quiet spell to free memory, and reload the moment you return.

Straight to tasks

Select text anywhere, press ⌘⇧T, and it is a task in the list on the right.

Brain break

Maths, words, memory and patterns. Two minutes for your own head between the things the machines do.

Built to stay private

Local first. Sandboxed by default. Updated on its own.

Nothing to sync

No Just Zen cloud. Notes, tasks, whiteboard and settings live on your Mac, Keychain-encrypted.

Apps can't see each other

Each website runs sandboxed in its own profile, with no route to your files or to Claude.

Claude is boxed in

Chat runs inside an OS sandbox. It reads the folder you chose and talks only to Anthropic.

Signed and self-updating

Signed with a Developer ID, notarized by Apple, and patched without you doing anything.

Just Zen is open source. Every line, the security review and the automated checks are public at github.com/kierankeene122/just-zen, so you can inspect exactly what runs on your Mac. The full picture, including what still depends on you, is on the Security page.
