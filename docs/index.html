A small, quiet launcher for Mac

Everything you use.
One flick away.

Just Zen is a panel on the edge of your screen and one key for everything else. Click Gmail and it opens in your browser — in the tab you already had open. Click Excel and the spreadsheet opens in Excel. Nothing moves house.

⌥Space

Two letters, from anywhere.

One field over everything the machine holds: your applications, your documents (Spotlight finds them, nothing to index), the web-app library, the sites you have added yourself, and the tabs already open in Safari or Chrome. Enter goes there. ⌘Enter puts it on the panel.

Your browser, not ours

It opens the tab you already had open.

Web apps go to Safari or Chrome — whichever your Mac opens links with — and Just Zen looks for the tab that already has that site before it makes another. Your profiles, extensions, passwords and sessions stay exactly where they are. Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Slack open through their own links, so you land inside the app.

Why a launcher and not another browser?

Because you already have a browser. And apps. And windows.

Nothing moves house

No new place to sign in, no second set of tabs, no copy of your working life kept somewhere else. Just a faster way to the things you already have.

It weighs almost nothing

A small panel and a key. No embedded browser running in the background, no pages kept warm, no memory quietly eaten while you work.

Claude, when you want it

Claude Code runs in its own window on the panel, sandboxed to the folder you choose, with your notes and tasks beside it. Nothing is sent anywhere until you ask.

Nothing leaves your machine

No account, no sync, no telemetry. Settings in the Keychain. Open source, so you can check.

Less hunting. Less switching. Less app.

Download it, pin six things, and press ⌥Space when you need anything else.
